# Style Rules

## What the sample covers share

- Big Chinese headline dominates the top area.
- 3:4 vertical layout, made for feed thumbnails.
- One expressive young subject is the anchor.
- A prop or screen makes the topic feel concrete.
- The background is often dark, glossy, studio-like, or event-like.
- Accent colors lean neon lime, bright white, yellow, orange, or cyan.
- The scene is dense: glow, arrows, UI chips, screenshots, film strips, or floating icons.
- The subject and headline stay readable at small size.

## Visual formula

1. Headline first.
2. Subject second.
3. Evidence layer third.
4. Decorative fragments last.

Keep the layout layered, not flat. Make the subject feel embedded in a live scene rather than pasted onto a blank background.

## Identity handling

When the user supplies portrait references:

- Use the same person as the cover subject.
- Preserve face shape, eye spacing, nose/mouth proportions, hair length, bangs, and accessory cues.
- Change only emotion, pose, clothing styling, and surrounding props as the topic needs.
- Prefer one canonical reference when several images conflict slightly.
- Keep the result recognizably the same person across repeated generations.

## Copy formula

Use one of these patterns:

- `主题 + 结果`
- `数字 + 方法 + 结果`
- `工具名 + 场景 + 收益`
- `反差/疑问 + 解决方案`

Good cover text is short, specific, and outcome-driven.

Preferred tone:

- 结果先行
- 方法明确
- 轻好奇
- 轻挑战
- 带一点“我发现了”

## Typography rules

- Use oversized bold Chinese characters.
- Add a dark stroke or heavy shadow.
- Keep the first line strongest.
- Use a second line only when it helps clarity or adds a payoff.
- A small badge can add structure, but do not let it compete with the main hook.

## Color and lighting

- Use high contrast.
- Let one accent color dominate the hook.
- Keep skin tones natural.
- Use glow, rim light, or screen light to separate the subject from the background.
- Favor glossy, cinematic, or tech-leaning lighting over flat daylight.

## Prompt scaffold

Write prompts in this order:

1. Topic and target outcome.
2. Subject description.
3. Main scene or setting.
4. Key prop or screen.
5. Headline style and placement.
6. Lighting and color treatment.
7. Composition constraints.
8. Negative prompt.

Example scaffold:

`A 3:4 vertical short-video cover about [topic], featuring [subject] in [scene], holding or pointing at [core prop], with a huge bold Chinese headline at the top, neon lime and white typography, layered UI fragments, high contrast cinematic lighting, expressive face, dense thumbnail composition, no logos, no watermark, no copyrighted characters.`

## Do not copy

- Real people
- Brand logos
- App UI trademarks
- Character IP
- Exact poster layouts from the samples
- Sample-specific props or faces

Recreate the energy, not the assets.
